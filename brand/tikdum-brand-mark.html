Concept

The check

A booking made, a provider verified, a job completed — the app's whole promise is a series of things confirmed. The checkmark is the literal shape of that promise.

The drop

Every one of these services happens at a real address, not in an app. The tail extending from the check reads as a pin drop — the moment the provider arrives.

The mark

One continuous stroke, no lettering to shrink — it reads the same as a 512px splash icon or a 16px browser tab, which is where it has to work hardest.
