Privacy Policy

Tikdum — Local Services Marketplace

Effective date: September 16, 2026  ·  This policy applies to the Tikdum customer app, the Tikdum Pro provider app, and tikdum.com.

Tikdum ("we", "us", "our") operates a local services marketplace that connects customers with independent service providers for home services such as AC repair, cleaning, and salon services. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, information generated by using the service, and information from your device.

  • Account information — name, phone number, email address, and profile photo.
  • Location — your address or device location, used to show nearby providers and enable accurate service dispatch.
  • Booking and service data — services booked, appointment times, booking status, messages exchanged with a provider or customer, and reviews/ratings you submit.
  • Payment and transaction records — booking amounts and payment status, to process and track your orders. We do not store full card numbers ourselves.
  • Device and push notification data — a push subscription token so we can send you booking updates and announcements even when the app isn't open, plus basic device/browser information.
  • Service provider details (Tikdum Pro users only) — business name, category, years of experience, service area, GST number where applicable, and identity/verification documents submitted for approval.

2. How We Use Information

  • To connect customers with nearby, relevant service providers and process bookings.
  • To send booking confirmations, status updates, and service-related notifications.
  • To send occasional announcements or offers from Tikdum, where permitted.
  • To verify service provider identity and maintain trust and safety on the platform.
  • To improve our services, prevent fraud, and resolve disputes or complaints.

3. Push Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store a device-specific push subscription so we can deliver booking updates and announcements directly to your device. You can withdraw this permission at any time from your device or browser notification settings; doing so stops future push delivery but does not affect your account.

4. Sharing of Information

We share the minimum information necessary to fulfill a booking — for example, a customer's address and contact number are shared with the assigned service provider, and a provider's name, rating, and contact details are shown to the customer. We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share information with service providers who help us operate the platform (such as hosting and messaging infrastructure), or where required by law.

5. Data Retention

We retain account and booking data for as long as your account is active and as needed to comply with legal, accounting, or dispute-resolution obligations. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

6. Your Rights

You can access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information by contacting us at support@tikdum.com. You can also update most profile details directly within the app.

7. Children's Privacy

Tikdum is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or how your data is handled, contact us at support@tikdum.com.
