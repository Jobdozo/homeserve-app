About Tikdum

Trusted home services across Jammu & Kashmir

Tikdum is a local services marketplace built for Jammu & Kashmir. We help you book trusted professionals for the jobs a home needs — from deep cleaning and AC repair to plumbing, electrical work, pest control and salon services — and make it easy for skilled local providers to find steady work.

What we believe

  • Trust comes first. Every provider on Tikdum is verified by our team before they can take a booking.
  • Prices should be clear. You see the price and any discount before you book.
  • Local matters. We show you providers near you, and we are growing area by area so that quality stays high.
  • Both sides deserve respect. Customers get protection and a place to raise problems; providers get fair requests, clear earnings and a way to build a reputation.

How Tikdum works

  • Choose a service and set your address.
  • Pick from nearby providers, compare prices and ratings, and book.
  • Your professional arrives at your door. Chat with them in the app, then rate the service.

Booking Protection

Bookings made through Tikdum are covered by our genuine service guarantee, damage protection up to ₹10,000, a fair price guarantee and emergency help. Read the details in our Terms.
