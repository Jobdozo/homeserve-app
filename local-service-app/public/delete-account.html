Delete your Tikdum account

How to delete your account and what happens to your data

Delete it yourself in the app

  1. Open the Tikdum app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile and tap Delete account at the bottom.
  3. Type DELETE to confirm.

You can't delete your account while you have an active booking — wait for it to finish or cancel it first.

Can't use the app? Ask us to delete it

Email support@tikdum.com from the email address on your account, or tell us the mobile number you signed up with. You can also call us on +91 94191 49336. We'll confirm it's you and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your name, profile photo, phone number and email address.
  • Your saved addresses and referral details.
  • The address, notes and chat messages on your past bookings.
  • Your notification settings and the devices registered for notifications.

What is kept

  • Records of completed bookings and payments, without your personal details, because tax, accounting and dispute rules require them.
